Our kitchen

Simple ingredients. Serious attention.

Aurelia is a fictional neighborhood dining room imagined for this demo. The menu changes with the market, but the idea stays the same: rich flavor, restrained technique, and plates that feel generous without being heavy.

Our imaginary chef, Mara Vale, spent a decade moving between coastal kitchens before opening this 38-seat room with a wood-fired hearth at its center.
